# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Quality Assurance & Testing Specialist

### Role Summary
QA/Testing professionals validate that features meet acceptance criteria and maintain quality standards throughout the delivery lifecycle. They work closely with developers and product managers to ensure product reliability and user satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
- Validate acceptance criteria and feature behavior
- Design and execute integration and end-to-end testing
- Oversee security scanning in CI and manage QA automation
- Report quality metrics and test coverage to the team
- Identify and triage bugs; verify fixes before release
- Collaborate with developers on test strategy and automation

### Goals
- Ensure product quality and user satisfaction
- Catch regressions and issues early
- Reduce post-release incidents and support burden
- Maintain high test coverage and automation standards

### Typical Communication
- Test results and quality metrics in sprint reviews
- Bug reports and verification in PR reviews
- QA approach and test plan in planning sessions
- Risk escalations on quality or blockers to PM and Tech Lead

---

## Tech Lead / Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads guide technical strategy and decisions, identify architectural risks, and mentor developers to ensure system quality, scalability, and maintainability. They serve as technical advisors to the Product Manager and Project Manager.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design decisions and architectural proposals
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and performance concerns
- Mentor developers and conduct technical reviews
- Advise PM on technical trade-offs and feasibility
- Ensure scalability, performance, and maintainability standards
- Contribute to technical documentation and design docs

### Goals
- Maintain high system quality and performance
- Reduce technical debt and risk
- Enable team efficiency and knowledge sharing
- Support informed decision-making on technical trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Technical design docs and architecture reviews
- Risk escalations to PM and Project Manager
- Guidance in planning and standups on feasibility and estimates
- Mentoring and code review feedback to developers

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide strategic business context, make priority and go/no-go decisions, and remove organizational blockers to ensure projects deliver business value. They represent senior leadership and business interests.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment
- Make go/no-go and priority decisions at project gates
- Approve resource allocation and resolve organizational blockers
- Receive and act on escalations from PM and project leadership
- Align project with broader business strategy and roadmap
- Communicate project status and outcomes to broader leadership

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Manage stakeholder expectations and maintain alignment
- Support team success and remove friction
- Drive strategic outcomes and competitive advantage

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and decision points
- Escalation notifications and resolution (P1 items)
- Kickoff and gate review meetings
- Executive summaries and business impact reports

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance professionals ensure projects meet security requirements, comply with organizational policies, and maintain customer trust. They integrate security into every phase of the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements during planning phase
- Conduct threat assessments and security risk reviews
- Oversee security scanning and compliance checks in CI/CD pipelines
- Review and approve release security checklists
- Lead incident communication for security-related issues
- Maintain security incident playbooks and runbooks
- Advise on secure design patterns and remediation of vulnerabilities

### Goals
- Minimize security risks and vulnerabilities
- Ensure compliance with organizational and regulatory standards
- Maintain customer trust and data protection
- Enable secure development practices across teams

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements documentation in planning phase
- Security review results and remediation guidance in sprint reviews
- Incident response coordination and communication
- Integration of security scanning feedback in CI processes
- Escalations to PM and Sponsor on critical security issues

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Personas interact collaboratively: Developers work with QA, Tech Leads guide architecture with PM input, Sponsors enable Developers through blockers, and Security integrates across all phases.
